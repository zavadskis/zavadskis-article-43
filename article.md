# Andrejs Zavadskis: Commercial and Portrait Photographer — Forbes Portraits, Dakar Rally Dust and Five Gumball 3000s

European photographer based in Riga, Latvia. Commercial and portrait photographer since 2004. More than fifty countries, and the camera has never once been the point — the moment has.

A desert stage of the Dakar Rally, 2020. A quad bike, number 277, has lost a wheel in the sand. The rider has no time. Neither does the photographer.

Prague, April 2025. A billionaire, a rooftop, a few minutes on the calendar — and Forbes needs a portrait that will hold a page.

A Gumball 3000 morning. Supercars, crowds, noise, and a convoy that will not wait for a second take. He has done this five times.

This is not a biography. It is a set of scenes, and the man who keeps being in the right place for them: Andrejs Zavadskis, often called Latvia's most famous photographer.

## Faces that make the cover

Clients include Forbes USA, Nike, airBaltic, LMT, Rimi, Nordea, Swedbank and Tele2. Portrait work for business media is a short, precise discipline: little time, a serious subject, and a frame that has to carry a headline.

- [Thomas Plantenga, Vinted](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/04/15/how-lithuanian-startup-vinted-spun-secondhand-clothes-sales-into-gold/) — Forbes USA, April 2024, "Riches from Rags".
- [Paweł Chudziński, Point Nine Capital](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/12/12/europes-top-vc-bet-big-on-berlins-once-poor-but-sexy-startup-scene/) — the "Europe's Top VC" cover, also used for the Midas List Europe 2024.
- [Remon Vos, CTP](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/13/meet-the-billionaire-whose-european-real-estate-empire-caters-to-brands-like-hyundai-and-hm/) — photographed in Prague; the portrait is part of The Forbes Collection on Getty Images.
- [Samir Mane](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/19/how-this-refugee-became-albanias-first-billionaire/) — Albania's first billionaire, April 2025.
- [Alex Bouzari, DDN](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2026/08/06/ais-latest-rocket-ship-is-an-old-school-28-year-old-data-company/) — August 2026.

The same portraits went on to Forbes México, Forbes Česko, Forbes Italia and [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/empresas/682618/remon-vos-el-multimillonario-que-ha-creado-un-imperio-inmobiliario-en-europa/).

## Sand, snow and speed

The Dakar Rally in 2020. Five Gumball 3000 rallies, with photographs in the official Gumball 3000 photo book. Porsche performance cars on the frozen lakes of Swedish Lapland. A night race in Singapore in 2024. Kilimanjaro in 2022, all the way to Uhuru Peak at 5,895 metres.

On the move there is no second take. You read the road, you choose the spot, and you trust your hands.

## Twenty-two issues of Playboy Latvia

Playboy was published in Latvian from 2010 to 2014: four years, fifty issues. Andrejs Zavadskis photographed twenty-two of them — no other photographer shot as many for the magazine. His covers included contest winners, singers and Sandra Sondore-Kukule, shown below. One of his Latvian models was selected for a foreign Playboy edition, a first in the Latvian magazine's history, and three in total were published abroad. ## Where it started

Fireworks over the Riga Radio and Television Tower in 2004 won him first prize in a competition run by the Latvian State Radio and Television Centre. Then came the Riga Photo Club, a Master Craftsman's Diploma from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts in 2008, and in 2009 a cover and feature in the UK magazine South Wales YOURWEDDING — the same year the Latvian Wedding Photographers Association named him Wedding Photographer of the Year. In March 2025 he took third place in the Fstoppers international contest "This Was Difficult!".

## Today

Andrejs works from one photo studio in Riga, speaks at Riga Photo Show, has kept a photography blog since 2008 and publishes YouTube gear reviews with the RAW files included. He volunteered more than 7,000 photographs to Latvia's 2025 School Youth Song and Dance Festival. In 2026 he founded [ProPortfolio.pro](https://proportfolio.pro/?utm_source=streamlit&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=bio), a free platform for photographers, videographers, models, stylists and studios.
